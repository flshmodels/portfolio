Ships made in Blender as miniature game units. I work from photos, then simplify the smaller fittings so the ship still reads clearly at a distance.

USS Cooperstown

Freedom class · Blender

I wanted the bridge and mast to be recognizable first, with more to look at up close: the boat bay, roof ladders, walkways and stern equipment. The rear mount took a few revisions to get its supports and proportions right.

The source parts stay separate in Blender for editing. The export combines them into seven meshes that share one small palette texture.

Type 148 Tiger

Patrol boat · Blender

This was the first boat in the set. I used early-service photos for the gun mounts and radar layout. Most of the cleanup was in the connections: fitting the launchers to their saddles, seating the rear mount and following the slope of the foredeck.

The export is five meshes with a shared palette texture.
